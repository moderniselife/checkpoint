# Getting help

- **Something broken?** Check [FEATURES.md → Troubleshooting](FEATURES.md#4-support--troubleshooting), then open a [bug report](https://github.com/moderniselife/checkpoint/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Want something new?** Look at the [idea board](ideas/IDEAS.md), then open a [feature request](https://github.com/moderniselife/checkpoint/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Need another AI provider or tracker?** Use the [AI provider](https://github.com/moderniselife/checkpoint/issues/new?template=ai_provider_request.yml) or [tracker](https://github.com/moderniselife/checkpoint/issues/new?template=tracker_request.yml) request forms.
- **Security issue?** Report it privately — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
