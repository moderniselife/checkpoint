# Code of Conduct

Checkpoint is a friendly, welcoming project. Everyone who participates — in issues, pull requests, discussions
or anywhere else — is expected to help keep it that way.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Assume good intent, and give constructive, specific feedback.
- Welcome newcomers and people of every background, identity and level of experience.
- Respect people's time and privacy — never share someone's personal information or private ticket data.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code and issues that break this code, and may warn, temporarily
restrict or permanently ban anyone who does so. Report problems privately to the maintainer via
[@moderniselife](https://github.com/moderniselife) or a [private security advisory](https://github.com/moderniselife/checkpoint/security/advisories/new)
if it's sensitive. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
