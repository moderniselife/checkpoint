# Tracking: tickets and research

**What:** the right-hand panel and the feed that show what Checkpoint looked at.
**Why:** trust comes from receipts — every claim in a plan should trace to something read.

## Ticket panel

Click any ticket key (feed, plan header, task groups, AC sources) or press **⌘I** for a resizable
glass panel with fields, time tracking, description, inline images, attachments with previews,
parent/sub-tasks/links, comments, work log, history and custom fields. Jira shows work log and
history; Linear shows project, cycle, estimate and link attachments.

- **Tabs**: open several tickets, switch with ⌃Tab, hide the panel with Esc — tabs persist.
- **Epic children** get a done/in-progress/to-do breakdown, filter, and **Plan each child**.
- Attachment previews use your API token where OAuth falls short (see Connections).

## Research feed and log

While a run works, the feed streams status, thinking and per-ticket tool chips (pending → ✓/⚠).
Long thinking expands in place. **The feed follows the bottom only while you're already there** —
scroll up to read and it stays put; scroll back down and it resumes following.

Every plan keeps its **research log** (Plan → Research tab): tickets read, lookups, thoughts,
duration — and the **model cost** readout (tokens + ≈$). Plans made before logging re-capture it
on re-run.
